# Downloaded Papers

All files below were opened with `pypdf`; page counts and EOF markers are checked
in the final validation. Detailed chunk manifests are in `papers/pages/`.

1. **Rainbow Connection of Random Regular Graphs**
   - Andrzej Dudek, Alan Frieze, Charalampos E. Tsourakakis (2015), SIAM Journal
     on Discrete Mathematics 29, 2255--2266; arXiv:1311.2299;
     DOI: 10.1137/140998433.
   - File: [1311.2299_rainbow_connection_random_regular.pdf](1311.2299_rainbow_connection_random_regular.pdf)
   - Relevance: proves `rc(G(n,d))=O(log n)` w.h.p. for fixed `d>=4`.

2. **Some Remarks on Rainbow Connectivity**
   - Nina Kamčev, Michael Krivelevich, Benny Sudakov (2016), Journal of Graph
     Theory 83, 372--383; arXiv:1501.00821; DOI: 10.1002/jgt.22003.
   - File: [1501.00821_some_remarks_rainbow_connectivity.pdf](1501.00821_some_remarks_rainbow_connectivity.pdf)
   - Relevance: edge-splitting proof of `O(log n/log d)` for fixed `d>=5` and an
     expander generalization.

3. **A Note on the Rainbow Connection of Random Regular Graphs**
   - Michael Molloy (2017), Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 24(3), P3.49;
     DOI: 10.37236/6789.
   - File: [molloy_2017_note_random_regular.pdf](molloy_2017_note_random_regular.pdf)
   - Relevance: settles the remaining cubic case with `rc(G(n,3))=O(log n)`.

4. **Rainbow Connection of Sparse Random Graphs**
   - Alan Frieze, Charalampos E. Tsourakakis (2012), Electronic Journal of
     Combinatorics 19(4), P5; arXiv:1201.4603; DOI: 10.37236/2784.
   - File: [1201.4603_rainbow_connectivity_sparse_random.pdf](1201.4603_rainbow_connectivity_sparse_random.pdf)
   - Relevance: first random-regular bounds and the explicit conjecture
     `rc(G(n,d))=diam(G(n,d))` w.h.p.

5. **Rainbow Connection Number and Connected Dominating Sets**
   - L. Sunil Chandran, Anita Das, Deepak Rajendraprasad, Nithin M. Varma
     (2012), Journal of Graph Theory 71, 206--218; arXiv:1010.2296;
     DOI: 10.1002/jgt.20643.
   - File: [1010.2296_connected_dominating_sets.pdf](1010.2296_connected_dominating_sets.pdf)
   - Relevance: proves the universal upper bound `3n/(delta+1)+3`; crucial for
     diagnosing the prompt's mistaken interpretation.

6. **On Rainbow Connection**
   - Yair Caro, Arie Lev, Yehuda Roditty, Zsolt Tuza, Raphael Yuster (2008),
     Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 15, R57; DOI: 10.37236/781.
   - File: [caro_et_al_2008_on_rainbow_connection.pdf](caro_et_al_2008_on_rainbow_connection.pdf)
   - Relevance: foundational minimum-degree bounds, extremal examples, and random
     graph threshold work.

7. **Rainbow Connections of Graphs -- A Survey**
   - Xueliang Li, Yuefang Sun (arXiv version, 2011), arXiv:1101.5747. The later
     journal survey is by Li, Shi, and Sun, Graphs and Combinatorics 29 (2013),
     1--38, DOI: 10.1007/s00373-012-1243-2.
   - File: [1101.5747_rainbow_connections_survey.pdf](1101.5747_rainbow_connections_survey.pdf)
   - Relevance: definitions, foundational results, variants, and complexity.
